About Sahyadri Trekking Portal

Our Mission

The Maharashtra Government Sahyadri Trekking Portal aims to promote responsible and safe trekking in the Western Ghats while preserving the natural beauty and historical significance of these ancient forts.

Why This Portal?

The Sahyadri range in Maharashtra is home to numerous historic forts and breathtaking landscapes. However, unregulated trekking has led to environmental degradation and safety concerns. This portal addresses these issues through:

  • Controlled crowd management with slot booking system
  • Real-time weather monitoring for safety
  • Preservation of historical sites
  • Emergency response system
  • Educational resources for responsible trekking

The Sahyadri Range

The Western Ghats, also known as Sahyadri, are a UNESCO World Heritage Site stretching across Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Kerala. In Maharashtra, these mountains are dotted with over 300 forts built by the Maratha Empire under Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

These forts not only offer spectacular views but also serve as a testament to the rich history and architectural brilliance of the Maratha Empire. Trekking through these forts provides an opportunity to connect with nature while experiencing history firsthand.

Key Features
