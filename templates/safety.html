Trekking Safety Guidelines

Pre-Trek Checklist

Before booking your trek, please ensure you have the following:

  • Valid trek pass (carried in printed form)
  • Government ID proof
  • Comfortable trekking shoes with good grip
  • Sufficient water (minimum 2 liters per person)
  • Energy snacks (nuts, fruits, energy bars)
  • First aid kit
  • Cap/hat and sunscreen
  • Raincoat or poncho (especially during monsoon)
  • Flashlight with extra batteries
  • Whistle for emergency signaling

During Trek Safety

While on the trek, follow these safety measures:

  • Always trek in groups, never alone
  • Follow the designated trail markers
  • Stay with your group and inform the guide if you need to step away
  • Carry sufficient water and consume regularly
  • Take regular breaks to avoid exhaustion
  • Be cautious during steep ascents and descents
  • Respect wildlife and maintain distance
  • Do not litter - carry a trash bag for your waste
  • Inform your guide immediately if you feel unwell

Weather Safety Guidelines

Safe Weather Conditions

Trekking is safe during clear skies and mild temperatures. Morning slots are recommended during hot weather.

Moderate Rain

Careful trekking possible with rain gear. Trails may be slippery. Cancellation depends on intensity.

Heavy Rain/Thunderstorm

Trekking is prohibited. All bookings will be automatically canceled with full refund.
